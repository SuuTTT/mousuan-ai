应用成果 · 04 / 结构化 AI

结构化 AI让大模型与智能体可理解

从编码树到大模型与智能体，把知识编辑、推理和行动放进可解释的结构信息框架。

01

结构化知识

模型不应只保存分散的事实。结构化 AI 关注知识之间的层级、关系和组织方式，使知识可以被定位、编辑与验证。

02

推理与行动

可解释推理需要展示信息如何被解码、哪些结构关系支持结论，以及结论如何转化为行动。

03

从模型到智能体

大模型提供广泛的语言与知识能力，结构信息为其补充可组织、可控制、可验证的内部结构，推动智能体走向自主与可信。

OUTCOMES · 04 / STRUCTURED AI

Structured AIwhen models and agents become legible

From encoding trees to foundation models and agents, structured AI places knowledge editing, reasoning, and action inside an interpretable structural-information framework.

01

Structured knowledge

A model should not only store disconnected facts. Structured AI studies hierarchy, relations, and organisation so knowledge can be located, edited, and verified.

02

Reasoning and action

Explainable reasoning shows how information was decoded, which structural relations support a conclusion, and how that conclusion becomes an action.

03

From models to agents

Foundation models bring broad language and knowledge capabilities. Structural information adds organisation, control, and verification so agents can become more autonomous and trustworthy.
