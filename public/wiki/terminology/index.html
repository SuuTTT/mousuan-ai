中文术语统一英文中文含义English meaning
谋算智能MouSuan Strategy (MSI)由“谋”与“算”两种互补策略结合生成的智能：谋进行层谱抽象的全局认知与推理、跨越抽象层谱的演算与推理并更新编码树；算进行计算与逻辑推理。The intelligence that is generated by combining Mou and Suan: Mou performs hierarchically abstracting global recognition and reasoning, calculating and reasoning that cross the hierarchies of abstracting, and encoding-tree updating; Suan performs computing and calculating as a strategy of logical reasoning.
智Strategically designing智能中的“智”：认知与策略设计的科学原理，负责从信息中形成可解释的策略设计。“Zhi” refers to the scientific principles of cognition and strategic design.
能Efficiently acting智能中的“能”：有效行动的工程原理与执行能力，把策略落实为可验证的行动。“Neng” refers to the engineering principles and capability for effective action.
智能Intelligence“智”与“能”的统一：既能进行策略设计，又能有效行动。智能需要行动。Intelligence, in the Chinese sense, is the unity of the two: strategically designing and efficiently acting. Intelligence requires action.
谋Mou层谱抽象的全局认知与推理；其数学模型是编码树，属于信息科学概念。Hierarchically abstracting recognition; calculating and reasoning that cross the hierarchies of abstracting; updating the encoding tree. Mou is a strategy of hierarchically abstracting global recognition and reasoning.
层谱抽象hierarchically abstracting跨越层级组织复杂世界，形成能够被观察、编码并表示为数据结构的组织。Organising a complex world across a spectrum of hierarchies so that it can be observed, encoded, and represented as a data structure.
编码树encoding tree层谱抽象的数学模型，用树结构表示从局部到整体的层级组织。The mathematical model of hierarchically abstracting, representing an organisation from local parts to the whole as a tree.
全局认知global recognition and reasoning从整体结构与关系出发认知并推理信息世界，而不只拟合局部相关性。Understanding and reasoning about the information world from the whole-system structure and relations, rather than from local correlations alone.
跨越抽象层谱的演算与推理calculating and reasoning that cross the hierarchies of abstracting在不同抽象层之间传递信息、进行演算与推理，使整体目标能够落到局部行动。Calculating and reasoning that cross the hierarchies of abstracting so that information and global objectives can reach local action.
算Suan计算与分而治之；属于经典数学、物理概念，把问题拆解并聚合结果。Computing and calculating, which is a strategy of logical reasoning.
计算Computing / Calculating对关系、状态与策略进行可执行、可验证的运算。Computing and calculating, which is a strategy of logical reasoning over relations, states, and strategies.
分而治之divide-and-conquer把复杂问题拆成子问题，分别计算，再组合为整体结果。Decomposing a complex problem, solving subproblems, and combining the results.
策略Strategy一种行动、操作或者算子。An action, an operation, or an operator.
生成策略Generating strategy生成信息的策略。A strategy that generates information.
解码策略Decoding strategy解码信息或消除不确定性的策略。A strategy to decode information or eliminate uncertainty.
公理化科学原理Axiomatized Science Principles以公理或定律、定义和可验证推论组织科学知识体系。A scientific knowledge system organised by axioms or law, definitions, and verifiable deductions.
信息科学information science研究信息的表示、演算、解码、生成及其在系统中的组织规律。The study of how information is represented, calculated, decoded, generated, and organised in systems.
谋算双脑体系结构MouSuan dual-brain architecture谋脑负责层谱抽象的全局认知与推理、跨越抽象层谱的演算与推理和编码树更新；算脑负责计算与逻辑推理，二者形成闭环。An architecture in which the Mou brain performs hierarchically abstracting global recognition and reasoning, calculating and reasoning that cross the hierarchies of abstracting, and encoding-tree updating, while the Suan brain performs computing and calculating as a strategy of logical reasoning.
谋算双脑体系结构智能机MouSuan dual-brain architecture intelligent machine以谋算双脑体系结构实现观察、解码、生成、计算、决策、行动与验证的智能机。An intelligent machine that observes, decodes, generates, calculates, decides, acts, and verifies with the MouSuan dual-brain architecture.
孙子模型The Sun Tzu Model谋算智能的模型：一个面向策略设计和有效行动的有原理模型。The Sun Tzu Model is a model of MouSuan Intelligence—a principled model for strategic design and effective action.

翻译约定：智是 Strategically designing，能是 Efficiently acting；智能是二者的统一，并且必须包含行动。Mou 表示层谱抽象的全局认知与推理、跨越抽象层谱的演算与推理和编码树更新；Suan 表示 Computing / Calculating，即逻辑推理策略；Mou、Suan 使用大写拼音，hierarchically abstracting、global recognition and reasoning、divide-and-conquer 使用小写；Axiomatized Science Principles 使用首字母大写。
